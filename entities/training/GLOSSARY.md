# Training

Taught sessions and the trainings people log for themselves: who was there, what they thought of
it, and what they were asked to do next.

## Attendance

**Register**:
The attendance rows of one session, one per person on it. It is the organiser's to keep.
_Avoid_: roster, attendance list

**Word**:
One statement of whether a person was at a session: the member's own, the organiser's, or a
transcript's reading. The latest word that is allowed to stand is the row's status.
_Avoid_: answer, confirmation, override

**Source**:
Whose word set a register row's status: the member, the organiser, or the transcript.

**Correction**:
A member's or organiser's word that differs from what the transcript read. A transcript never
replaces a correction.
_Avoid_: override

**Window**:
The time a member's word and review stay open: seven days from the session's end, or from the
moment they logged it when they logged it late. After it the member's word is final; the
organiser's is not.
_Avoid_: grace period, deadline

**Review**:
A member's short rating of a session they went to. It exists only on a row whose status is
attended, and only the member may remove it.
_Avoid_: feedback, rating

**Signed note**:
A review's note that its member chose to put their name on. The name is never stored on the
review: it is read when the note is shown, and only while the member is on the team (the team
gate's statuses). A leaver's note reads nameless; their review still counts.
_Avoid_: attributed note, named feedback

**Leaver**:
A person who can no longer open the team hub. Nothing is written when someone leaves: their
reviews stay in the programme read's counts, and their signed notes lose the name (TR.11).
_Avoid_: ex-member, offboarded

**Outcome**:
Something a person achieved, in their own words, on the day it happened, with the trainings
and plans they credit for it (TR.6). Only they read it. A leaver's outcomes stay on their person
record: nothing deletes a person (TR.11).
_Avoid_: result, win, KPI

**Suggestion**:
An outcome offered to a person by an organiser or by a certificate they earned, waiting for
their word. It counts only once they add it; dismissed, it is kept so it is never offered again,
and whoever suggested it never learns which.
_Avoid_: nomination, recommendation

**Programme read**:
The figures for how trainings are landing, from reviews and attendance together: stars, the
load mix, feelings, who went and why not, and the notes. Each viewer reads only their scope
(Admin every training, a manager their team's, an organiser their own, a member their own
plans), and never one person's row (TR.7).
_Avoid_: report, analytics, dashboard

**Slice**:
One cut of the programme read: a training type or a plan, over a period. A slice fewer than
three people reviewed, or one that would let a reader subtract their way to fewer than three,
is hidden with the reason, for everyone.
_Avoid_: segment, group, bucket
