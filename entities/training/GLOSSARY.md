# Training

Taught sessions and the trainings people log for themselves: who was there, what they thought of
it, and what they were asked to do next.

## Attendance

**Register**:
The attendance rows of one session, one per person on it. It is the organiser's to keep.
_Avoid_: roster, attendance list

**Word**:
One statement of whether a person was at a session: the member's own, the organiser's, or a
transcript's reading. The latest word that is allowed to stand is the row's status.
_Avoid_: answer, confirmation, override

**Source**:
Whose word set a register row's status: the member, the organiser, or the transcript.

**Correction**:
A member's or organiser's word that differs from what the transcript read. A transcript never
replaces a correction.
_Avoid_: override

**Window**:
The time a member's word and review stay open: seven days from the session's end, or from the
moment they logged it when they logged it late. After it the member's word is final; the
organiser's is not.
_Avoid_: grace period, deadline

**Review**:
A member's short rating of a session they went to. It exists only on a row whose status is
attended, and only the member may remove it.
_Avoid_: feedback, rating
