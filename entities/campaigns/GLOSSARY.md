# Campaigns

Marketing's posts across the blog, email and social channels, the calendar they are planned on, and the
images made for them.

## Posts and the calendar

**Post**:
One piece of content for one channel on one date: a blog article, an email, or a LinkedIn, Facebook or X post.
_Avoid_: Asset (in user-facing copy), entry

**Status**:
Where a post stands: idea, drafted, approved, scheduled, published, or not doing. Every status is kept, whatever
the board shows.
_Avoid_: Stage

**Draft column**:
The calendar board's first column, holding every post that is an idea, drafted or approved.
_Avoid_: Backlog

**Posted by hand**:
A social post the marketer publishes herself on the platform; only the blog publishes by itself.
_Avoid_: Manual publish, auto-post (for social)

**Ready to post**:
The one-click step for a post that is posted by hand: its text copied and its images downloaded in the shape the
platform takes.
_Avoid_: Export, share

## Images

**Code-drawn set**:
The images for a social post, drawn by code from the brand's look with the real logo file, at the channel's size.
_Avoid_: Template images, generated images

**Code-drawn hero**:
The one picture for a blog post or an email: its words and figures written by the model from the post, drawn by
code in the brand's hero colours with the real logo in a corner no words reach.
_Avoid_: Model art, AI image, Gemini image, banner art

**Figure**:
One number on a code-drawn hero with its short label. Its value is always one the post itself states.
_Avoid_: Stat, metric

**Look**:
The design a code-drawn set is drawn in: Signature, Editorial or Campaign set. Each brand has its own version of a
look, or none yet.
_Avoid_: Layout, style (for this sense), template

**Number of images**:
How many images a code-drawn set holds: 4, 2 or 1.
_Avoid_: Layout

**Style DNA**:
A brand's written visual rules, approved on its Visuals tab, which the writer reads when it drafts image briefs. A code-drawn picture's colours and type come from the brand kit, not from Style DNA.
_Avoid_: Brand prompt
