# Ladders

Two private climbs a person makes by acting, and the cosmetics they choose to wear from them.
Ladders is its own context: it reads the acts Quests, Spark, the Workboard and Training record,
through their doors, and turns them into Points. Nothing in those contexts levels, ranks or
totals a person; the ladders do, for the person alone, behind a door that answers only the
reader about the reader (ADR 0021). "Standing" keeps its Quests meaning (one Party member on one
quest); a person's place on a ladder is a Tier, a Division or Star, and Points, and the record of
it is a Ladder position.

## Ladders

**Ladder**:
A private climb a person makes by acting, from the bottom Tier upwards, only ever up. There are
two, so nobody is measured on a scale built for someone else's job.
_Avoid_: leaderboard, ranking, level, scale

**Learn & Share ladder**:
The ladder for learning and sharing: Turn-ins on Bounties, sparks and learnings, Raids hosted,
Beacons lit. Its Emblem is a Helm and it is worn as a Border.
_Avoid_: learning score, culture ladder

**Builder ladder**:
The ladder for work shipped on the Workboard: leaf cards moved to Done, by their Human Tokens. Its
Emblem is a Medal.
_Avoid_: delivery score, velocity, output

**Act**:
A thing a person did that earns Points: a Turn-in, a spark, a Held fire hosted (Quests' word: its
session ended and someone other than the Host came), a Beacon lit, a leaf card shipped. Applause is not an Act: a Reaction, a Helped or a kudos earns
nothing.
_Avoid_: activity, contribution, event

**Points**:
What a person's Acts add up to on one ladder, by a fixed table. Seen by the person alone. Points
only go up through Acts; a corrected source row may lower them, but never the Tier.
_Avoid_: XP, score, karma

**Tier**:
A named band of Points on one ladder (Gold, Archon), starting at a fixed number of Points that
never depends on anyone else. A Tier once reached is kept. It is the only part of a person's
place that anyone else can ever see, and only when it is Worn.
_Avoid_: rank (as a noun about a person), level, grade, league

**Division**:
One of four equal parts of a Learn & Share Tier, IV up to I. Seen by the person alone. Sovereign
has none.
_Avoid_: sub-rank, step

**Star**:
One of five equal parts of a Builder Tier, one Star on reaching it. Seen by the person alone.
Immortal has none.
_Avoid_: pip, level

**Sovereign**:
The open top of the Learn & Share ladder: one Tier with no Divisions and no ceiling.
_Avoid_: champion, top player

**Immortal band**:
One of the three medals Immortal wears as its Points grow. From outside, all three are simply
Immortal; a band is never a position among people.
_Avoid_: top 10, top 100, leaderboard rank

**Ladder position**:
The record of where one person is on one ladder: their Points, Tier and Division or Stars. It can
always be rebuilt from the Acts behind it.
_Avoid_: standing, score, rank

**Rank-up**:
The full-screen moment when a person reaches a new Tier, shown to them alone where the Act
landed, only ever upwards.
_Avoid_: promotion, level up

## Wearing

**Emblem**:
The art of one Tier: a Helm on Learn & Share, a Medal on Builder.
_Avoid_: icon, badge, logo

**Helm**:
A Learn & Share Emblem: an armoured helmet, one per Tier, Iron to Sovereign.

**Medal**:
A Builder Emblem: a winged medal, one per Tier, Herald to Immortal, with its Stars over the top.

**Border**:
The ring round a person's face in the Learn & Share Tier they Wear. A face with no Border says
nothing: it never means no Tier.
_Avoid_: frame, outline, halo

**Wear**:
To choose to show a Tier, a Title or a Badge to teammates. Only the person wears or removes, and
everything starts unworn. Worn things are seen by teammates only, never on the client portal.
_Avoid_: publish, share, display (as a verb), equip

**Badge**:
A named thing found by doing, separate from the ladders. Never lost, never taken away, and Worn
only by choice.
_Avoid_: achievement points, trophy, award

**Locked**:
A Badge not found yet, seen by its owner alone with the plain rule that finds it. A Locked Badge
is a door, not a debt.
_Avoid_: missing, not earned, incomplete

**Secret badge**:
A Badge whose Locked tile shows only a hint, never its rule.
_Avoid_: hidden achievement, easter egg

**Rarity**:
How hard a Badge is to find, as a word set by its design: common, uncommon, rare, epic,
legendary. Where a live share is shown it is a band (held by many, by some, by a few), and only
once three people hold it; never a percentage.
_Avoid_: percentile, drop rate

**Display case**:
The five Badges a person chooses to show on their profile.
_Avoid_: showcase, trophy shelf, wall

**Sticker**:
A Badge worn on the postcard; at most three.
_Avoid_: pin, flair

**Title**:
A name a person chose to wear under theirs on the profile, taken from the Badges they found.
_Avoid_: rank, role, job title

**Banner**:
The scene a person picks for the top of their profile: a place from the Quests world, or their
own photo.
_Avoid_: cover, header image

**Plaque**:
The dark panel on the profile holding the face with its Border, the Title and the two Emblems
side by side, each shown or a dash.
_Avoid_: card, badge, scorecard
