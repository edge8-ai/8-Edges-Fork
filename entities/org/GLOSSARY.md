# Org

The people side of the company: who is on the team, where they sit, and the reviews written
about their work.

## Reviews

**Manager of record**:
The person inside the company a team member reports to. They write the company's own reviews of
that member.
_Avoid_: manager (alone), line manager

**Framework**:
A named set of review questions and the scale they are answered on, with one form for the member
and one for whoever rates them. A framework belongs to no client and no team; a cycle picks one.
_Avoid_: template, form (for the whole set), instrument

**Client review**:
A review of a team member placed at a client, held on the client's own form at the client's own
times of year. The member rates themselves and their client manager rates them.
_Avoid_: external review, 360, partner review

**Client manager**:
The person at the client who directs a placed team member's daily work and rates them in a
client review. They are a contact of the client, never a team member.
_Avoid_: manager (alone), external reviewer

**Skip-manager**:
The person at the client whom the client managers answer to. They read a client review and may
comment on the client manager's answers; they rate nobody.
_Avoid_: approver, second reviewer

**Cycle**:
One holding of a client review at one client: the members reviewed, each one's client manager,
and the skip-manager, from the day it is opened to the day it is closed.
_Avoid_: round, season, review period

**HR summary**:
A note the person running a cycle writes about one member's client review. It sits beside the
client manager's answers and never replaces them.
_Avoid_: consolidated review, revised review

**Release**:
The moment the person running a client review lets the member it is about see what their client
manager wrote. Until then the member sees only their own answers.
_Avoid_: finalise, publish, share
